Artist Classification Using a Convolutional Neural Network

Predicting which of five artists painted an image, using a TensorFlow/Keras CNN. The report records 82.5% test accuracy.

Overview

Given an image of a painting, the model predicts one of five artists: Andy Warhol, Pablo Picasso, Rembrandt, Salvador Dalí or Vincent van Gogh. It learns visual patterns from labelled examples using a convolutional neural network (CNN).

Developed with Andrej Praizović for a neural networks course. The work covers dataset preparation, a CNN built in TensorFlow/Keras, training and evaluation on unseen paintings.

The artwork dataset

Images come from the Best Artworks of All Time (Kaggle) dataset, reduced to five artists. The report describes a dataset of 880 paintings across five classes. Inputs are resized to 128 × 128 RGB pixels and rescaled to [0, 1].

To give the model more varied examples, training randomly flips, rotates and zooms the paintings. Data augmentation, dropout and early stopping help reduce overfitting: learning the training images too closely to recognise new ones.

Model & training

The CNN has three convolutional layers with 16, 32 and 64 filters to learn image features, followed by a 128-unit dense layer. Two max-pooling layers reduce image dimensions. The final softmax layer assigns a probability to each of the five artists. The model has 8,412,965 trainable parameters.

Training uses Adam and sparse categorical cross-entropy, a loss for choosing among labelled classes. Hidden layers use ReLU activations. The training and validation curves still show a performance gap, so overfitting remains a limitation.

Results & limitations

82.5%

Test accuracy reported in Figure 21
Five artist classes

The model correctly classified 82.5% of test paintings. The confusion matrix shows which artists it mixes up: Van Gogh is most often confused with Picasso, and some Warhol paintings are also labelled Picasso. Recall is highest for Dalí (100%) and Rembrandt (96%).

The model can choose only among these five artists. It cannot authenticate a painting or recognise an artist outside the training classes. More varied data and transfer learning are possible next experiments.
