Music discovery through play

Slavuj is a web app for discovering music through creative games, solo quizzes and group games with friends. Turn your Spotify favourites into games, discover new tracks and take them back to your library.

I came up with the idea and built it with Dušan Korizma, Mark Možarovski and Marko Misovski for our Principles of Software Engineering course in 2026.

Getting started

Your first visit

The demo follows the landing page, Spotify authorisation and game selection. Connecting an account enables personalised music suggestions and playlist export.

Games & demos

Build playlists in three different ways, or test your knowledge of artists, albums and genres. These demos show each game in action.

Cover Paint

Pick a colour and explore matching album covers. Select tracks from the results to build a playlist around a visual theme.

Secret Message

Write a message, then choose songs whose title initials spell it out. Save the finished playlist to Spotify or share it with someone.

Playlist Snake

Build a chain where each song title starts with the last word of the previous title. Play Snake along the way and keep the tracks as a playlist.

Top 5

Try to name an artist’s five most-streamed songs. Correct guesses appear in the ranking. Reveal the remaining tracks to discover what you missed.

More solo quizzes

Imposter asks you to spot the odd song, artist or album. Fan Check tests your knowledge of artists, bands, albums and genres.

How well do you know your friends’ music?

Create a room and invite friends with a code or invitation. Rounds draw on the players’ music tastes, with scores to compare and a shared playlist to save afterwards.

Music Roulette
Recognise tracks from your friends’ favourites.
Imposter
Find the song that does not belong.
Hitster
Put songs in chronological order.
Quiz Blitz
Race through a music quiz together.

Profile & playlists

Your music library

Revisit saved playlists, inspect their tracks and browse previous games. The profile also brings together friends and play statistics.

How it is built

Python and Django handle the server logic, while HTML, CSS and JavaScript power the interface and games.

Accounts & sessions
Django manages sign-in and sessions, with local accounts stored in Firestore.
Music & game data
Firestore stores profiles, music data, game state, friendships and playlists.
Spotify API
The Spotify Web API supplies music data and saves playlists to the user’s connected account.

Software engineering documentation

Our team wrote the requirements, use cases, diagrams and database specification. These describe user flows, alternative paths and data relationships, giving us a shared plan for implementation.
